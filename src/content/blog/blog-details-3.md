The newly formed Vapi Municipal Corporation (VMC) in 2025 marks a major shift from ad‑hoc growth to organised urban development. The approval of the city’s first Town Planning (TP) scheme unlocks planned road networks, better plot shapes and dedicated land for utilities, hospitals, gardens and affordable housing. For businesses, this means clearer regulations, better infrastructure and a more attractive environment for long‑term investment. At the same time, Gujarat’s industrial and GCC policies continue to back Vapi as a competitive industrial hub, supported by strong logistics, skilled workforce and connectivity to major metros.

Looking ahead, planned upgrades such as improved expressway links, the upcoming bullet train corridor between Mumbai and Ahmedabad, and regional air connectivity through the Daman airport are expected to further compress travel times and widen Vapi’s catchment for talent, trade and customers. In this context, demand is shifting from basic industrial sheds to premium commercial spaces that can match the expectations of modern entrepreneurs, professionals and brands.

## NeoVista: crafted as Vapi’s iconic commercial landmark

![NeoVista twin towers rising over Char Rasta, Vapi](../../assets/images/neovista-towers.jpg)

NeoVista is a RERA‑registered, all‑commercial project at Vapi, conceived as a twin‑tower landmark offering offices, showrooms, hospitality and healthcare‑ready spaces under one address. Located within Vapi Municipality and planned for completion in the current development cycle, it brings a vertical business district experience that the city has not seen before. Large glass façades, a strong street presence and premium common areas are designed to position NeoVista as the reference point for Char‑Rasta and its surroundings.

For businesses, NeoVista offers a curated mix of unit sizes and typologies—from compact offices for professionals to high‑frontage retail floors and specialised spaces that can house clinics or wellness centres. The project’s design language and amenity stack are aligned with the needs of modern brands: grand entrance lobby, efficient cores, advanced fire‑safety systems and 5‑star style common areas that elevate everyday work life.

## Odhav Developers’ vision for NeoVista and Vapi

Odhav Developers has been active in South Gujarat for over two decades, with a stated focus on reliability and long‑term value creation rather than short‑term hype. With NeoVista, the developer’s vision is to give Vapi an address that reflects the city’s new ambition — an integrated business hub where local entrepreneurs, regional brands and national players can operate from the same ecosystem. The idea is simple: if Vapi is stepping up in terms of industrial competitiveness and civic infrastructure, its commercial spaces should also speak the language of scale, design and professionalism.

NeoVista is therefore positioned not only as a real estate product, but as a symbol of Vapi’s transition from an industrial town to a full‑fledged business city. By combining thoughtful planning, credible execution and a premium brand experience, Odhav Developers aims to anchor future commercial growth around this landmark.

## Strategic connectivity: Char‑Rasta, expressways and upcoming links

Location is the strongest lever in commercial real estate, and NeoVista’s siting near Vapi Char‑Rasta gives it immediate visibility as well as access. Char‑Rasta functions as one of the city’s key junctions, linking the GIDC industrial estate, residential neighbourhoods and the wider regional road network. Being within quick reach of National/State highways and the expressway network that connects Vapi to Mumbai, Surat and Vadodara allows businesses operating from NeoVista to service multiple markets from a single base.

Forward‑looking infrastructure adds another layer of advantage. The Mumbai–Ahmedabad high‑speed rail corridor and the broader expressway upgrades in South Gujarat are expected to bring faster business travel and logistics, while the Daman airport project is set to enhance regional air connectivity for executives and tourists alike. For companies and professionals, this means NeoVista can function as a highly accessible “middle ground” between major metros and manufacturing clusters.
